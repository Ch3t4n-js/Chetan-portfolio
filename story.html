A deeper look into what brought me to Mechanical Engineering, my academic trajectory, the activities that energize me, and achievements along the way.

Why Mechanical Engineering?

Growing up, I was never satisfied by just knowing that a machine worked—I was obsessed with understanding how every tooth on a gear, every pivot point, and every pressure valve contributed to motion. Whether it was disassembling broken clocks, fine-tuning bicycle derailleurs, or building cardboard hydraulic arms, physical mechanics felt like an intuitive universal language.

“Engineering isn't merely formulas on paper; it is the discipline of giving elegant, durable form to physical laws.”

Now in my first year of B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, that childhood fascination has matured into structured academic inquiry. My freshmen semesters have introduced me to the rigor of vector statics, computer-aided drafting (CAD) in AutoCAD, and hands-on workshop practices like metal fitting and lathe operations.

Beyond the classroom, I am deeply interested in the future convergence of mechanical design with computational automation, robotics, and clean energy mobility. Every day in the lecture hall and workshop is a deliberate step toward becoming an engineer capable of designing solutions that stand the test of real-world physics.
